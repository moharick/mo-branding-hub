# MO Branding Studio

Create a modern, professional, responsive business website for a company called “MO BRANDING EXPERTS”.

Use the uploaded MO Branding Experts logo as the primary brand logo. Use the uploaded services poster as the reference for the company’s services, but redesign the website with a cleaner, more premium and modern layout.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Company name: MO BRANDING EXPERTS
- Tagline: EXPERTS
- Primary brand color: deep navy blue from the logo
- Supporting colors: white, light grey, and subtle navy accents
- Overall style: professional, modern, clean, trustworthy and corporate
- Avoid excessive animations, gradients, or clutter.
- Use strong typography, generous spacing, rounded cards and high-quality relevant imagery.
- Make the website fully responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE:

1. HOME / HERO SECTION
Create a strong hero section with:
“Professional Solutions. One Trusted Partner.”
Supporting text:
“From career documents and business branding to graphic design, websites and government services, MO Branding Experts provides professional solutions tailored to your needs.”

Add two CTA buttons:
- “View Our Services”
- “Contact Us”

Display the company logo prominently.

2. ABOUT US
Create a concise About section explaining that MO Branding Experts provides professional branding, career documentation, design, digital and administrative services for individuals, businesses and organizations.

Include a simple “Why Choose Us” section with:
- Professional & Reliable
- Tailored Solutions
- Quality Work
- Convenient Service
- One-Stop Business Support

3. SERVICES
Create a visually attractive services section divided into categories.

CATEGORY 1 — RESUME & LINKEDIN
- ATS-Optimized Resumes and CVs
- Modern and Professional Resumes
- LinkedIn Profile Optimization
- Tailored Cover Letters
- Job Portfolios
- Recommendation Letters
- Personal Statements
- Executive Bios

CATEGORY 2 — DESIGN & DOCUMENTS
- Posters
- Flyers
- Certificates
- Business Plans
- Proposal Writing
- Logos
- Business Cards
- Social Media Graphics
- Menus
- Invitation Cards

CATEGORY 3 — WEBSITE & DIGITAL SERVICES
Add website building as a major service.
- Professional Website Design
- Business Website Development
- Landing Pages
- Portfolio Websites
- Responsive Mobile-Friendly Websites
- Website Redesign
- Website Content Setup
- Basic Website Maintenance

CATEGORY 4 — GOVERNMENT SERVICES
- KRA Tax Returns
- E-Citizen Services
- Passport Applications
- Good Conduct Applications
- NTSA Services
- Business Registration
- Birth & Death Certificate Applications

For each service category, use a professional icon or relevant image and a short description. Add a “Get Started” or “Request Service” button.

4. FEATURED SERVICE — WEBSITE BUILDING
Create a dedicated section promoting website development.

Headline:
“Take Your Business Online”

Text:
“We design professional, responsive websites that give your business a strong online presence and make it easier for customers to find, understand and contact you.”

Include:
- Modern design
- Mobile responsive
- Fast and user-friendly
- Professional business presentation
- Contact and inquiry forms
- Social media integration

CTA:
“Build My Website”

5. OUR PROCESS
Create a simple 4-step process:
1. Tell Us What You Need
2. Get a Quote
3. We Create & Refine
4. Receive Your Final Product

6. PORTFOLIO / OUR WORK
Create a portfolio/gallery section showcasing examples of:
- Logos
- Posters
- Business cards
- Resumes
- Social media graphics
- Websites

Use clean cards with category filters if appropriate.

7. WHY CHOOSE US
Create a professional section highlighting:
“Everything You Need. Under One Brand.”

Explain that clients can access career services, branding, design, websites and government-related services from one provider.

8. CONTACT SECTION
Create a clear contact section with:
Email: mobrandingexperts@gmail.com
Phone: +254 721 517 679

Include:
- Contact form
- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Service Required
- Message

Add a prominent WhatsApp contact button.

9. FOOTER
Include:
MO BRANDING EXPERTS logo
“Professional Solutions. One Trusted Partner.”

Navigation links:
Home
About
Services
Portfolio
Contact

Include the phone number, email and social media icons.

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Fully responsive
- Mobile-first design
- Smooth scrolling
- Working contact form
- WhatsApp CTA
- Clickable phone number and email
- Clear navigation
- SEO-friendly page structure
- Fast loading
- Accessible typography and contrast
- Professional hover effects
- Subtle animations only where useful

IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION:
Do NOT simply copy the uploaded poster. Use it as a content reference and transform the information into a premium modern website.

The website should feel like a professional Kenyan digital services and branding company rather than a generic template.

Make the homepage visually strong, with the services immediately visible and clear CTAs throughout the page.

Use the uploaded MO Branding Experts logo throughout the website and maintain the logo’s navy-blue identity.
